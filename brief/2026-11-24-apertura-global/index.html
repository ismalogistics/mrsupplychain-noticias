Apertura

El premercado anticipaba un ajuste tras el rebote del lunes y la apertura lo confirmó en tecnología: a las 9:08 CDMX el S&P 500 estaba en 7,189.57 (−0.66%), el Nasdaq en 23,713.45 (−1.56%) y el Dow en 49,791.96 (+0.40%); el VIX en 27.5. El índice de semiconductores caía 7.3%: AMD −9.1%, Micron −9.1%, Nvidia −6.8% (153.85 dólares), TSMC −4.5% y Broadcom −3.1%.

Anthropic caía 7.9% a 80.70 dólares, debajo de su precio de salida de 83, pese a que Canaccord, Oppenheimer y Wedbush iniciaron cobertura con recomendación de compra, según 24/7 Wall St. HPE perdía 15% tras su guía, Dell 11.7% antes de reportar, y CoreWeave y Nebius cerca de 10%. Best Buy subía 1.7% tras reportar ventas comparables de +6.1%, su mejor alza en cinco años, y Dick's Sporting Goods 13.8%.

Tasas y materias primas

El WTI rebotaba 1.8% a 81.64 dólares y el Brent de enero 1.8% a 90.93, desde mínimos de tres meses; el diésel en futuros estaba en 3.69 dólares por galón. El oro bajaba 0.8% a 3,624, la plata 2.6% y el cobre 3.3% a 5.53 dólares por libra.

El Treasury a 10 años estaba en 4.91% y el índice del dólar bajaba 0.52% a 103.78.

Asia, Europa y qué sigue

Asia cerró al alza: KOSPI +6.32% y Nikkei +3.91%. A las 8:53 CDMX Europa estaba mixta: Euro Stoxx 50 −0.54%, DAX −0.66%, CAC 40 +0.25% y FTSE 100 +0.13%.

A las 9:00 CDMX salió la confianza del consumidor del Conference Board; después del cierre reportan Dell y HP. Mañana salen el PCE y el PIB de EE. UU., y el jueves no opera Wall Street.

Para supply chain

El consumo de electrónica en EE. UU. aguanta (Best Buy +6.1% en ventas comparables): confirma inventario, recolecciones y cortes de embarque antes del miércoles 25. En hardware, escucha hoy a Dell y HP antes de cerrar pedidos de servidores o laptops.

Open

Premarket pointed to a pullback after Monday's rebound and the open confirmed it in tech: at 10:08 a.m. ET the S&P 500 stood at 7,189.57 (−0.66%), the Nasdaq at 23,713.45 (−1.56%) and the Dow at 49,791.96 (+0.40%); the VIX at 27.5. The semiconductor index fell 7.3%: AMD −9.1%, Micron −9.1%, Nvidia −6.8% ($153.85), TSMC −4.5% and Broadcom −3.1%.

Anthropic fell 7.9% to $80.70, below its $83 IPO price, even though Canaccord, Oppenheimer and Wedbush initiated coverage with buy ratings, according to 24/7 Wall St. HPE lost 15% on its guidance, Dell 11.7% ahead of its report, and CoreWeave and Nebius about 10%. Best Buy rose 1.7% after reporting comparable sales of +6.1%, its best gain in five years, and Dick's Sporting Goods 13.8%.

Rates and commodities

WTI rebounded 1.8% to $81.64 and January Brent 1.8% to $90.93, from three-month lows; diesel futures stood at $3.69 a gallon. Gold fell 0.8% to $3,624, silver 2.6% and copper 3.3% to $5.53 per pound.

The 10-year Treasury yield stood at 4.91% and the dollar index fell 0.52% to 103.78.

Asia, Europe and what’s next

Asia closed higher: KOSPI +6.32% and Nikkei +3.91%. At 9:53 a.m. ET Europe was mixed: Euro Stoxx 50 −0.54%, DAX −0.66%, CAC 40 +0.25% and FTSE 100 +0.13%.

The Conference Board's consumer confidence index came out at 10:00 a.m. ET; Dell and HP report after the close. US PCE and GDP come out tomorrow, and Wall Street is closed on Thursday.

For supply chain

US electronics demand holds up (Best Buy comparable sales +6.1%): confirm inventory, pickups and shipping cutoffs before Wednesday the 25th. In hardware, listen to Dell and HP today before closing server or laptop orders.

O rendimento do Treasury de 10 anos estava em 4,91% e o índice do dólar caía 0,52% para 103,78.

Ásia, Europa e o que vem

A Ásia fechou em alta: KOSPI +6,32% e Nikkei +3,91%. Às 8h53 (Cidade do México) a Europa estava mista: Euro Stoxx 50 −0,54%, DAX −0,66%, CAC 40 +0,25% e FTSE 100 +0,13%. Às 9h00 saiu a confiança do consumidor do Conference Board; depois do fechamento divulgam resultados Dell e HP. Amanhã saem o PCE e o PIB dos EUA, e na quinta Wall Street não opera.

Para supply chain

O consumo de eletrônicos nos EUA resiste (vendas comparáveis da Best Buy +6,1%): confirme estoque, coletas e prazos de embarque antes da quarta, dia 25. Em hardware, ouça hoje Dell e HP antes de fechar pedidos de servidores ou laptops.
