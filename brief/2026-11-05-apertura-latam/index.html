Apertura

Con datos de las 9:12 CDMX, el fondo de Argentina (ARGT) caía 5.29%, el de Perú 1.8%, el de Chile 1.53% y el S&P/TSX de Canadá 0.71% (36,083.48); el Ibovespa estaba plano (167,495.55, −0.04%) y el fondo de Colombia apenas subía 0.08%. El peso chileno se debilitaba a 971.9 por dólar, el colombiano a 3,231, el real a 5.47 y el sol a 3.45.

Contexto

La venta de chips y de infraestructura de IA en Wall Street (Nasdaq −3%) se contagia a los mercados emergentes. El rebote del petróleo a 90 dólares ayuda a Petrobras y a Colombia.

Ayer el Copom mantuvo la Selic en 13.75% y terminó el ciclo de recortes.

Qué se espera hoy

En México decide Banxico a las 13:00 CDMX. En Brasil, el mercado sigue digiriendo el comunicado del Copom.

Para supply chain

Las monedas de la región ceden parte de lo ganado ayer: si tienes pagos en pesos chilenos, soles o pesos colombianos, la ventana de ayer era mejor; no adelantes más hoy. Con la Selic en 13.75%, revisa plazos de pago con proveedores brasileños.

Opening

With data as of 9:12 a.m. Mexico City time, the Argentina fund (ARGT) fell 5.29%, the Peru fund 1.8%, the Chile fund 1.53% and Canada's S&P/TSX 0.71% (36,083.48); the Ibovespa was flat (167,495.55, −0.04%) and the Colombia fund barely rose 0.08%. The Chilean peso weakened to 971.9 per dollar, the Colombian peso to 3,231, the real to 5.47 and the sol to 3.45.

Context

The chip and AI infrastructure selloff on Wall Street (Nasdaq −3%) spills over to emerging markets. Oil's rebound to $90 helps Petrobras and Colombia.

Yesterday Copom held the Selic at 13.75% and ended the cutting cycle.

What to expect today

In Mexico, Banxico decides at 1:00 p.m. Mexico City time. In Brazil, the market keeps digesting the Copom statement.

For supply chain

The region's currencies give back part of yesterday's gains: if you have payments in Chilean pesos, soles or Colombian pesos, yesterday's window was better; don't pull forward more today. With the Selic at 13.75%, review payment terms with Brazilian suppliers.

O peso chileno se enfraquecia a 971,9 por dólar, o colombiano a 3.231, o dólar a R$ 5,47 e o sol a 3,45.

Contexto

O que se espera hoje

No México, o Banxico decide às 13h (Cidade do México). No Brasil, o mercado segue digerindo o comunicado do Copom.

Para supply chain

As moedas da região devolvem parte do ganho de ontem: se você tem pagamentos em pesos chilenos, soles ou pesos colombianos, a janela de ontem era melhor; não antecipe mais hoje. Com a Selic em 13,75%, revise prazos de pagamento com fornecedores brasileiros.
