Cómo va

Con los datos de las 9:30 CDMX, el Ibovespa cae 1.59% (165,260), el S&P/TSX de Canadá 0.58% (35,594.27) y los fondos de Perú (−0.92%), Chile (−0.61%) y Argentina (−0.59%) también bajan; el COLCAP sube 0.3% (24,535) al reabrir. En monedas, el real se debilita 1.2% a 5.50 por dólar y el peso mexicano a 18.72, mientras el chileno se mantiene fuerte (981.7).

Lo que sorprendió

Colombia fue la única bolsa de la región en positivo al reabrir. El real cruzó los 5.50 por dólar, con la cautela fiscal en Brasil y el Copom en reunión; la decisión de la Selic se anuncia mañana.

What was expected

The opening brief saw Brazil reopening with the Ibovespa down 1.45% and the real near 5.50, and the region starting in the red with the US election in the background.

How it is going

With data as of 9:30 a.m. Mexico City time, the Ibovespa is down 1.59% (165,260), Canada's S&P/TSX 0.58% (35,594.27) and the Peru (−0.92%), Chile (−0.61%) and Argentina (−0.59%) funds are lower too; the COLCAP rises 0.3% (24,535) as it reopens. In currencies, the real weakens 1.2% to 5.50 per dollar and the Mexican peso to 18.72, while the Chilean peso stays strong (981.7).

What surprised

Colombia was the only market in the region in positive territory on reopening. The real crossed 5.50 per dollar on fiscal caution in Brazil with Copom meeting; the Selic decision is announced tomorrow.

For supply chain

With the real above 5.50, payments to Brazilian suppliers in reais get cheaper for those paying in dollars, but sales to Brazil earn less: wait for tomorrow's Copom decision before setting quarterly prices.

Como está

Com os dados das 9h30 (Cidade do México), o Ibovespa cai 1,59% (165.260), o S&P/TSX do Canadá 0,58% (35.594,27) e os fundos do Peru (−0,92%), Chile (−0,61%) e Argentina (−0,59%) também caem; o COLCAP sobe 0,3% (24.535) na reabertura. Nas moedas, o real se enfraquece 1,2%, a R$ 5,50 por dólar, e o peso mexicano a 18,72, enquanto o chileno segue forte (981,7).

O que surpreendeu

A Colômbia foi a única bolsa da região no positivo na reabertura. O dólar passou de R$ 5,50 com a cautela fiscal no Brasil e o Copom reunido; a decisão da Selic sai amanhã.

Para supply chain

Com o dólar acima de R$ 5,50, os pagamentos a fornecedores brasileiros em reais ficam mais baratos para quem paga em dólares, mas as vendas ao Brasil rendem menos: espere a decisão do Copom de amanhã antes de fixar os preços do trimestre.
