⏱The essentials in 30 seconds

  • At 5:58 a.m. ET Nasdaq 100 futures were down 2.53%, S&P 500 futures 1.51% and Dow futures 0.77%; in premarket trading Nvidia fell 5.3% and Dell 7.4% despite record results.
  • AP and the New York Post report the US is preparing tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies over industrial subsidies; according to the Post, the list includes Mexico. Rates and timing are not confirmed.

Lo que pasó desde el cierre

Ayer el Nasdaq cerró con −1.48% y el índice de semiconductores con −7.9%, aunque el Dow volvió arriba de 50,000. Después del cierre, Dell reportó ingresos por 54,900 millones de dólares, contra 49,330 millones esperados, elevó su guía del año a 202,000 millones y mostró una cartera de pedidos de IA de 132,300 millones, pero su acción caía en preapertura, según Yahoo Finance.

AP, Bloomberg y The Wall Street Journal reportaron que EE. UU. prepara aranceles contra China, la UE y otras 14 economías tras una investigación por subsidios industriales; según el New York Post, la lista incluye a México, Japón y Corea del Sur, con tasas que no están definidas. En Washington crece la presión para restringir el acceso de China a chips avanzados tras el regreso de Jensen Huang, según Reuters.

Asia, Europa y futuros

Asia cerró a la baja: KOSPI −6.16% (4,697.51), Nikkei −2.78% (57,011.62), Hang Seng −1.87% y Shanghái −1.77%. A las 4:53 CDMX Europa estaba mixta: Euro Stoxx 50 −0.01%, DAX +0.17%, CAC 40 +1.02% y FTSE 100 +0.50%.

A las 4:58 CDMX los futuros del Nasdaq 100 bajaban 2.53%, los del S&P 500 1.51% y los del Dow 0.77%; el VIX subía a 29.4. En preapertura, Nvidia caía 5.3% (147.20 dólares), Micron 5.9%, AMD 5.7% y Dell 7.4%, según Investing.com. El WTI bajaba 0.9% a 81.35 dólares y el Brent de enero 0.6% a 90.89; el oro bajaba 0.9%, la plata 1.8% y el cobre 1.0% a 5.40 dólares por libra.

Qué se espera hoy

A las 7:30 CDMX salen en EE. UU. el índice de precios PCE de octubre (se espera que la medida subyacente suba a 3.2% anual), el PIB del tercer trimestre, los pedidos de bienes duraderos y las solicitudes de subsidio por desempleo. A las 13:00 CDMX la Fed publica el Libro Beige.

Deere reporta antes de la apertura. El mercado da 98% de probabilidad a un alza de la Fed en diciembre.

Mañana jueves no operan las bolsas de EE. UU. y el viernes la sesión es corta.

Para supply chain

Revisa hoy qué partes de tu cadena dependen de China, la UE, Japón o Corea, y si exportas a EE. UU. desde México, prepara escenarios de arancel: las tasas aún no se conocen. Deja hoy cerrados los embarques y recolecciones a EE. UU., que mañana es feriado.

What happened since the close

Yesterday the Nasdaq closed −1.48% and the semiconductor index −7.9%, although the Dow returned above 50,000. After the close, Dell reported revenue of $54.9 billion versus $49.33 billion expected, raised its full-year guidance to $202 billion and showed an AI backlog of $132.3 billion, but its stock fell in premarket trading, according to Yahoo Finance.

AP, Bloomberg and The Wall Street Journal reported the US is preparing tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies after an investigation into industrial subsidies; according to the New York Post, the list includes Mexico, Japan and South Korea, with rates not yet set. In Washington, pressure is growing to restrict China’s access to advanced chips after Jensen Huang’s return, according to Reuters.

Asia, Europe and futures

Asia closed lower: KOSPI −6.16% (4,697.51), Nikkei −2.78% (57,011.62), Hang Seng −1.87% and Shanghai −1.77%. At 5:53 a.m. ET Europe was mixed: Euro Stoxx 50 −0.01%, DAX +0.17%, CAC 40 +1.02% and FTSE 100 +0.50%.

At 5:58 a.m. ET Nasdaq 100 futures were down 2.53%, S&P 500 futures 1.51% and Dow futures 0.77%; the VIX rose to 29.4. In premarket trading, Nvidia fell 5.3% ($147.20), Micron 5.9%, AMD 5.7% and Dell 7.4%, according to Investing.com. WTI fell 0.9% to $81.35 and January Brent 0.6% to $90.89; gold fell 0.9%, silver 1.8% and copper 1.0% to $5.40 per pound.

What to expect today

At 8:30 a.m. ET the US releases October PCE prices (core is expected to rise to 3.2% annual), third-quarter GDP, durable goods orders and jobless claims. At 2:00 p.m. ET the Fed publishes its Beige Book.

Deere reports before the open. Markets see a 98% chance of a December Fed hike.

US markets are closed tomorrow, Thursday, and Friday is a shortened session.

For supply chain

Review today which parts of your supply chain depend on China, the EU, Japan or Korea, and if you export to the US from Mexico, prepare tariff scenarios: rates are not yet known. Close out US shipments and pickups today, since tomorrow is a holiday.
