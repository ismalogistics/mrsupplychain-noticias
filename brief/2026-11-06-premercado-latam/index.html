Contexto

Qué se espera hoy

El empleo de octubre en EE. UU. (7:30 CDMX) marcará el tono para las bolsas y monedas de la región. Brasil sigue con la Selic en 13.75% y México con la tasa en 6.25% tras el recorte de ayer.

What happened since the close

On Thursday the Argentina fund closed −5.66%, the Chile fund −3.25%, the Peru fund −3.12%, Canada's S&P/TSX −2.03% (35,605.27), the Ibovespa −0.82% (166,191.45) and the Colombia fund −0.88%, on the global AI selloff.

Context

Overnight the real was at 5.50 per dollar, the Chilean peso at 977.7, the Colombian peso at 3,237.9, the sol at 3.44 and the Argentine peso at 1,544.5. The Nikkei fell 6% in Asia and US futures are mixed.

What to expect today

The US October jobs report (7:30 a.m. Mexico City time) will set the tone for the region's stocks and currencies. Brazil keeps the Selic at 13.75% and Mexico its rate at 6.25% after yesterday's cut.

For supply chain

The region's currencies are weaker than at the start of the week: if you have local-currency receivables, convert them soon; payments in those currencies can wait until Monday.
