What was expected

The opening brief saw the region rebound with the US: the Chile fund +3%, Canada +1.3% and the Ibovespa +0.8% before the Copom decision.

How it is going

With data as of 10:38 a.m. Mexico City time, the Chile fund (ECH) is up 3.33%, the Peru fund 1.85%, the COLCAP 1.78% (25,055.5), Canada's S&P/TSX 1.74% (36,152.25), the Argentina fund 0.79% and the Ibovespa 0.68% (166,651.7). In currencies, the Chilean peso stays strong (969) and the real is at 5.49 per dollar.

What surprised

Peru and Colombia, laggards at the open, joined the rally. The Ibovespa trimmed its gain ahead of the Copom decision, and the real did not follow the dollar's global weakness.

For supply chain

With the whole region up and the dollar weak, Latin American currencies strengthen: review today which local-currency payments are worth pulling forward. Wait for this afternoon's Copom before settling non-urgent reais.
