Qué pasó

Con datos de las 13:04 CDMX, el fondo de Argentina (ARGT) caía 6.62%, el de Chile 3.61%, el de Perú 2.56%, el S&P/TSX de Canadá 2.06% (35,593.01), el fondo de Colombia 1.5% y el Ibovespa 0.62% (166,521.2).

Por qué

La venta de chips e infraestructura de IA en Wall Street (S&P 500 −4.4%) se contagió a los mercados emergentes. El Ibovespa resistió mejor por el peso de Petrobras y las materias primas, con el petróleo al alza.

Monedas

Por la tarde el peso chileno estaba en 978.4 por dólar, el colombiano en 3,237, el real en 5.49 y el sol en 3.443. El peso mexicano cerró en 18.75 tras el recorte de Banxico.

Qué se espera

La región abrirá sensible al empleo de EE. UU. de mañana. Brasil sigue con la Selic en 13.75% y México con la tasa en 6.25% y guía de pausa.

Para supply chain

Las monedas de la región se debilitan: si tienes cobros en monedas locales, conviértelos pronto. Con el petróleo cerca de 98 dólares (Brent), revisa los recargos de flete en rutas con Colombia, Perú y Chile.

What happened

With data as of 1:04 p.m. Mexico City time, the Argentina fund (ARGT) fell 6.62%, the Chile fund 3.61%, the Peru fund 2.56%, Canada's S&P/TSX 2.06% (35,593.01), the Colombia fund 1.5% and the Ibovespa 0.62% (166,521.2).

Why

The chip and AI infrastructure selloff on Wall Street (S&P 500 −4.4%) spilled over to emerging markets. The Ibovespa held up better thanks to Petrobras and commodities, with oil rising.

Currencies

In the afternoon the Chilean peso was at 978.4 per dollar, the Colombian peso at 3,237, the real at 5.49 and the sol at 3.443. The Mexican peso closed at 18.75 after Banxico's cut.

What to expect

The region will open sensitive to tomorrow's US jobs report. Brazil keeps the Selic at 13.75% and Mexico its rate at 6.25% with pause guidance.

For supply chain

Regional currencies are weakening: if you have local-currency receivables, convert them soon. With Brent near $98, review freight surcharges on routes with Colombia, Peru and Chile.

O que se espera

A região vai abrir sensível ao relatório de emprego dos EUA de amanhã. O Brasil segue com a Selic em 13,75% e o México com a taxa em 6,25% e sinalização de pausa.

Para supply chain

As moedas da região se enfraquecem: se você tem recebíveis em moeda local, converta logo. Com o Brent perto de 98 dólares, revise as sobretaxas de frete nas rotas com Colômbia, Peru e Chile.
